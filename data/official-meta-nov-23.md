## Data provenance and use
These **species-level official results are research references**, not simulated matches or validated complete team imports. Do not silently substitute guessed held items, move sets, or Stat Points. If using these teams as simulation opponents, obtain their full official open team sheets, verify legality, and identify any inferred spreads explicitly.

The machine-readable refresh in `data/field-recent.json` comes from public **Limitless online tournaments** and should **not** be described as containing all official Regionals or International Championship teams.
